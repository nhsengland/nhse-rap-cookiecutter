# Guides

Welcome! These short tutorials teach the good habits that make a data-science
project pleasant to work on — yours, and anyone who picks it up after you.

You don't need to read them all at once. They come in three parts, and each
guide ends with a link to the next, so you can work straight through or dip in
wherever you need.

## Part 1 — Understand your project

What each part of the generated project is, what it does, and why it's there.
Start here to get your bearings.

1. **[Project structure & why it matters](01-project-structure.md)** —
   what every folder is for, and why a tidy layout saves you time.
2. **[A tour of your project's files](02-understanding-your-project.md)** —
   the key files explained, so nothing feels like a black box.

## Part 2 — Basic skills for working in the repo

The everyday skills you need to actually do the work — and a short introduction
to RAP, the practice this whole template is built around.

3. **[Virtual environments](03-virtual-environments.md)** —
   why you don't install packages "globally", and how venv & pip help.
4. **[Pandas basics](04-pandas-basics.md)** —
   the handful of DataFrame operations you'll use again and again.
5. **[From notebooks to modules](05-notebooks-to-modules.md)** —
   when to move code out of a notebook into reusable files.
6. **[Reading data safely](06-reading-data-safely.md)** —
   loading files in a way that fails clearly instead of mysteriously.
7. **[Making your first plot](07-making-a-first-plot.md)** —
   turning a table of numbers into a chart you can save and share.
8. **[Git basics](08-git-basics.md)** —
   saving your work in small, labelled steps you can go back to.
9. **[Using GitHub](09-using-github.md)** —
   backing up and sharing your project online.
10. **[Keeping data & secrets safe](10-keeping-data-and-secrets-safe.md)** —
    the golden rule: keep code public, data private.
11. **[What is RAP?](11-what-is-rap.md)** —
    Reproducible Analytical Pipelines, and the levels you can aim for.

## Part 3 — Going further (towards full RAP)

The more advanced habits that make your work reliable, reproducible, and ready
to grow into a full production pipeline.

12. **[Writing your first test](12-writing-a-first-test.md)** —
    proving your code does what you think, automatically.
13. **[Writing good tests](13-writing-good-tests.md)** —
    tests that catch real bugs, not just boost the count.
14. **[Functional coding](14-functional-coding.md)** —
    building your work out of small, well-named functions.
15. **[Logging](15-logging.md)** —
    the grown-up alternative to scattering `print` everywhere.
16. **[Bringing in uv](16-bringing-in-uv.md)** —
    an optional, faster upgrade to your environment tooling.
17. **[Bridging to the full RAP template](17-bridging-to-the-full-rap-template.md)** —
    what the production RAP template adds, and how to step up.
18. **[What good looks like](18-what-good-looks-like.md)** —
    a friendly checklist to glance at before you share your work.

---

Each guide is self-contained and uses the code already in this project as its
example. Take your time, and tinker as you go — breaking things and fixing them
is how this sticks.

When you're ready to share your code publicly, also work through
[`OPEN_CODE_CHECKLIST.md`](../OPEN_CODE_CHECKLIST.md) in the project root.
